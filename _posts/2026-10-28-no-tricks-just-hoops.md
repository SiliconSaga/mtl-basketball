Happy Halloween from MTL Basketball! No tricks here, just a reminder: registration for the Winter 2026–27 season closes **November 10**.

There are boys and girls leagues for elementary (grades 4–5) and middle school (grades 6–8), and the fee is $135 per player. Practices start in December and games in January.

Register for the [elementary league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81079) or the [middle school league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81126), or start at the [registration page]({{ '/register/' | relative_url }}).

Middle school players also come to one short [evaluation night]({{ '/evaluations/' | relative_url }}) at Edison Middle School, any time 6:30–8:30 pm. The last one is Tuesday, November 10.
