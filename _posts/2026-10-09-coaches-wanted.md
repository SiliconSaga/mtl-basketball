Every MTL coach is a volunteer: parents and neighbors who said yes. Could the open spot be yours?

- About two practices a week
- The league provides basketballs, first aid kits, a coach's bag, and the gym
- First-time MTL coaches complete a background check

Interested? Fill in the short [coaches form](https://forms.gle/pEtTjuadSrFYRGPs7), or read more on [Get Involved]({{ '/get-involved/' | relative_url }}).

Registration is still open: $135, boys and girls leagues for grades 4–8, closing November 10. Middle school [evaluation nights]({{ '/evaluations/' | relative_url }}) start October 19 at Edison Middle School. Everything is on the [registration page]({{ '/register/' | relative_url }}).
