MTL Basketball registration is open until November 10, with boys and girls leagues for elementary (grades 4–5) and middle school (grades 6–8). The fee is $135 per player. The season runs December through March, with games starting in January.

Register for the [elementary league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81079) or the [middle school league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81126), or start at the [registration page]({{ '/register/' | relative_url }}).

Middle school players come to one short [evaluation night]({{ '/evaluations/' | relative_url }}) at Edison Middle School, any time 6:30–8:30 pm: October 19, 21, 27, 29, or November 10.

We're still looking for volunteer coaches, too: see [Coaches wanted]({{ '/news/2026/10/coaches-wanted/' | relative_url }}) or go straight to the [coaches form](https://forms.gle/pEtTjuadSrFYRGPs7).
