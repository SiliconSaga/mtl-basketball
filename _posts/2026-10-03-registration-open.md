MTL Basketball registration is open for Winter 2026–27, with boys and girls leagues for elementary (grades 4–5) and middle school (grades 6–8) players. MTL Basketball is an inclusive in-house league for every skill level: local teams, games in town, a focus on skill development and learning the game the right way, and above all making sure everyone has a blast.

- $110 early bird through October 4, then $135
- Registration closes November 10
- The season runs December through March: practices aim to start the first or second week of December, and games begin in January

Register for the [elementary league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81079) or the [middle school league](https://registration.teamsnap.com/form/81126), or read the [registration page]({{ '/register/' | relative_url }}) first.

Middle school players attend one short [evaluation night]({{ '/evaluations/' | relative_url }}) (about 15–20 minutes, arrive any time 6:30–8:30 pm) so we can make balanced teams. Pick any date at Edison Middle School: October 19, 21, 27, 29, or November 10.

Made the West Orange Travel Team and want to play MTL as well? West Orange Rec will send you a discount code once the team is set.

Every coach is a volunteer. Want to [coach](https://forms.gle/pEtTjuadSrFYRGPs7) or [sponsor](https://forms.gle/vi9jTD5b2vFZ6EQD8) a team? Questions go to [basketball.mtl@gmail.com](mailto:basketball.mtl@gmail.com).
