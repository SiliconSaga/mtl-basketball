## Answered

- **Grades:** Elementary is grades 4–5; Middle School is grades 6–8.
- **Number of games:** varies — the coordinators asked that it NOT be published.
- **Season:** December through March.
- **Game days:** elementary on Sunday afternoons, middle school Monday–Thursday; all subject to change based on participation.
- **Gyms:** West Orange school gyms; weekday practices; elementary Sunday games at varying locations.
- **Teams:** middle school balanced from evaluations; elementary formed mainly by school, depending on participation; friend and coach requests not considered.
- **What to bring:** workout or gym clothes and sneakers, for the season and the evaluation night. Basketballs are provided.
- **Coaching:** practices set by coach availability, about two nights a week on average; background check required for first-time MTL coaches, and a recent check from another MTL sport should still be valid; the league provides basketballs, scorebooks, first aid kits, cones, coach's bags, and gym or practice space.
- **Sponsorship:** levels and benefits are all on the sponsorship form.
- From the original announcement: fees, deadlines, evaluation dates and window, travel-team discount via West Orange Rec, coach and sponsor forms, program description.
