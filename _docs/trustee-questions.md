# Questions for the basketball trustees

What the site still needs from the coordinators, grouped by the page it unblocks. Every item below also appears on the live page as a **Coordinator input needed** callout, so answering one lets the callout come down. The soccer and hockey sites went through the same list; the items marked *(soccer/hockey have this)* are where basketball is behind its siblings.

## Registration and leagues (`_data/divisions.yml`, Register, How It Works)

1. Which grades does the **Elementary** league cover, and which the **Middle School** league? K–5 and 6–8, or something else? *(soccer/hockey have this)*
2. Can a player play up or down a level (a strong 5th grader in middle school, say)? Who decides?
3. Is there a minimum age or grade?
4. Are the boys and girls leagues separate registrations inside the same TeamSnap form, or does the form ask? (The site currently says "each league has a boys division and a girls division".)
5. Is there a sibling discount, a payment plan, or financial aid? Hockey advertises monthly payments.
6. How does the West Orange Travel Team discount work exactly: a percentage, a fixed amount, applied at checkout or refunded?
7. What happens to a late registration after November 10 — waitlist, or closed?

## Season shape (How It Works, FAQ)

8. How many games in a season, and how many practices a week? *(soccer/hockey have this)*
9. Which day(s) of the week do practices and games land on, and roughly what times? Weeknights, Saturdays, Sunday afternoons?
10. When does the season end? Is there a playoff or a championship day? (The Drive folders of playoff photos suggest yes.)
11. How long is a game — quarters, halves, running clock?
12. Are there referees, and are they volunteers, paid, or high-school students? (Affects the sportsmanship wording.)
13. Is score kept and are standings posted, at each level?

## Teams and evaluations (Evaluations, How It Works)

14. How are **elementary** teams formed — by school, by grade, by evaluation, randomly?
15. Are friend or coach requests considered at either level?
16. What should a player wear and bring to the evaluation night? Which door at Edison, and where to park?
17. What if a middle school player can't make any of the five evaluation dates?
18. Roughly how many players per team, and how many teams per league last season? (Helps parents gauge playing time.)

## Gyms (Gyms pages) *(soccer/hockey have this)*

19. Which gyms host **practices** and which host **games**, for each league? Each confirmed gym gets a page with a map.
20. Edison Middle School's street address for the map link, plus entrance and parking notes.
21. Any gym rules parents should know — spectator seating, no outside food, sneakers only on the floor?

## Gear and uniforms (How It Works, FAQ) *(soccer/hockey have this)*

22. What does the league provide — a reversible jersey, a t-shirt? Does the player keep it?
23. What should a player bring — sneakers, a ball (what size per league), water?
24. Anything required or banned — mouthguards, jewelry, glasses straps?

## Coaching and sponsorship (Get Involved)

25. What does coaching involve week to week? Is a background check, a concussion course, or any training required?
26. What does the league give a coach — a bag, balls, a practice plan?
27. Sponsorship levels and what a sponsor gets — jersey placement, a banner, a mention on the site?

## Communications and identity

28. Is basketball.mtl@gmail.com the only public contact, and who reads it? (Hockey has one address; the coordinators' personal emails stay off the site.)
29. Does the league want a `basketball.mountaintopleague.com` subdomain like soccer and hockey? That needs one DNS record from whoever manages the domain; the site is ready for the flip.
30. Is there a WordPress `/basketball` page on mountaintopleague.com that the flyers should point at, or should they point at the new site once it has a domain?
31. Photos: may we use the 25–26 and 24–25 playoff photos from the shared Drive folders on the site and flyers? Any players whose families have asked not to be pictured?
32. Anything from last year's blast wording the trustees want kept word for word?
