## The League

MTL Basketball is an inclusive in-house league for players of varying skill levels. Teams are made up of West Orange kids and play other West Orange teams, in town. The focus is skill development and learning the game correctly — and making sure everyone has a blast doing it.

## Making Teams

Middle school teams are built from the evaluation nights so the league stays balanced. Everyone running MTL is a volunteer, coaches included, and many of them are barely more than another parent who was handed a bag of gear and a schedule — so please give the team-making process some grace.

> **Coordinator input needed:** how elementary teams are formed (by school? by grade?), and whether friend or coach requests are considered.

## On the Sideline

Two requests that keep game day enjoyable for the kids:

- **Give it 24 hours.** If you disagree with a coaching decision, wait a day before raising it with the coach, and do it away from the kids.
- **Please don't approach a referee about a call.** If you believe a game was officiated in a way the league should hear about, email [basketball.mtl@gmail.com](mailto:basketball.mtl@gmail.com) or use the [league contact form](https://mountaintopleague.com/contact-us/).

The full [24-hour rule](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/sportsmanship/), and the rest of what we ask of coaches and parents, lives on the [MTL Volunteering site](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/).
