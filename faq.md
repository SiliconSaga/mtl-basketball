## Which league is my child in?

There are two, each with a boys division and a girls division: **Elementary School** for grades 4–5 and **Middle School** for grades 6–8. Register for the one that matches your child's grade this school year — see [How It Works]({{ site.baseurl }}/how-it-works/).

## When are games and practices?

The season runs roughly {{ site.data.season.season_span }}. Elementary games are usually on Sunday afternoons and middle school games on weeknights, Monday through Thursday. Practices are on weeknights, typically about two a week depending on the coaches' availability. Days and times may shift depending on how many players register.

## Can my child be on a team with a friend, or with a particular coach?

Friend and coach requests aren't taken. Elementary teams are formed mainly by school, and middle school teams are balanced using the evaluation nights.

## How much does it cost?

{{ site.data.season.fee }} per player. Current numbers and sign-up links are always on the [Register]({{ site.baseurl }}/register/) page.

## When does registration close?

{{ site.data.season.close_date }}. Practices aim to start in {{ site.data.season.practices_start }} and games begin in {{ site.data.season.games_start }}.

## Does my child need to attend an evaluation?

Only middle school players, and only **one** night — pick any of the five dates on the [Evaluations]({{ site.baseurl }}/evaluations/) page, arrive any time between 6:30 and 8:30 pm, and plan on about 15–20 minutes. It's how the coordinators make balanced teams, not a tryout. Elementary players skip this step.

## My child made the West Orange Travel Team. Can they also play MTL?

Yes. Travel-team players who want to play MTL Basketball too get a discount code, sent directly by West Orange Rec once the travel team is set.

## Where are games and practices?

In West Orange school gyms; the specific gym can vary through the season. Evaluations are at [Edison Middle School]({{ site.baseurl }}/gyms/edison/). See [Gyms]({{ site.baseurl }}/gyms/).

## What does my child need to bring?

Workout clothes and sneakers. The league provides the basketballs.

## I disagree with a call or a coaching decision. What should I do?

Give it 24 hours. MTL asks parents to wait a day before raising a concern about a coaching decision with the coach, away from the kids, and asks that nobody approach a referee about a call. If you believe a game was officiated in a way the league should hear about, email [basketball.mtl@gmail.com](mailto:basketball.mtl@gmail.com) or use the [league contact form](https://mountaintopleague.com/contact-us/). The full [24-hour rule](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/sportsmanship/) is on the MTL Volunteering site.

## Can I help?

Yes — MTL is all volunteers. There are quick interest forms for [coaching and sponsoring]({{ site.baseurl }}/get-involved/), and the cross-sport [MTL Volunteering guide](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/) covers what to expect.
